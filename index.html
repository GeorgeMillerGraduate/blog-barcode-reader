HOW IT WORKS

From pixels to data.

The scanner locates the symbol, samples its modules, applies error correction and reconstructs the encoded information.

01

Load an image or capture frames from a live camera feed.

02

Locate the structural patterns that identify the code.

03

04

Decode

Convert recovered codewords into the original text or URL.
